## 1. Purpose & Background

Step 7 closes the v0.1 model action surface with a player/NPC `Starship` and the single-player `TurnManager`. The paired contract fixes constructor and action validation, AP ownership, mutation-before-event ordering, Dock behavior, defensive player resolution, and the runtime dependency edges required by nominal type checks.

## 2. Scope

### 2.1 In Scope

- A keyword-only, defensively validated `Starship(GameObject)`.
- Atomic AP debit, single-cell movement, Dock, and unconditional AP restoration.
- A validated `TurnManager` with the approved End Turn pipeline.
- Exact action errors, synchronous event payloads, mutation order, and failure atomicity.
- Package exports, focused tests, runtime-DAG reconciliation, and documented future concerns.

### 2.2 Out of Scope (Non-Goals — never)

| ID | Non-Goal | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NG-001 | `TurnManager` shall not own AP, NPC initiative, or a combat roster. | AP belongs to each entity and v0.1 has one player turn. |

| NG-003 | `Starship` shall not import `TurnManager`. | The runtime dependency must remain one-directional and cycle-free. |

## 8. Architecture and Design

### 8.1 Architecture Summary

`Starship` is a mutable `GameObject` that runtime-imports Station for nominal Dock resolution and uses type-only SectorMap/EntityId imports. `TurnManager` runtime-imports Starship for defensive resolution and uses type-only SectorMap/EntityId imports. Both emit through the centralized model event bus after mutation, and neither imports Qt or creates a runtime backedge.

### 8.2 Architecture Views

#### 8.2.1 Context View

The wire-up layer owns a SectorMap, Starship, and TurnManager. User commands call Starship actions; End Turn calls TurnManager; the controller bridge consumes the emitted model events.

#### 8.2.2 Container / Deployment View

Both modules run in-process in the local desktop application and headless test processes; neither is independently deployed.

#### 8.2.3 Component View

| Component | Responsibility | Interfaces | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `entities/starship.py` | Scalar validation, AP ownership, movement, Dock, restoration. | `Starship`. | Runtime Station check; type-only SectorMap. |
| `combat/turn_manager.py` | Player resolution and End Turn ordering. | `TurnManager`. | Runtime Starship check; type-only SectorMap and EntityId. |
| `model.events` / exceptions | Typed outcomes. | Existing SPEC-S005 surface. | Mutate before emit. |
| Focused tests | Behavior, ordering, payload, and DAG evidence. | pytest, import-linter, BasedPyright. | Current repository gate uses coverage.py. |

### 8.3 Design Decisions

| ID | Decision | Rationale | Alternatives Considered | ADR |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D-001 | Keep AP on Starship behind one atomic `_debit_ap()` helper. | Every action shares one validated check-then-debit contract. | TurnManager-owned AP and duplicated inline debit logic were rejected. | — |
| D-002 | Validate every Starship scalar before `GameObject` allocates identity. | Invalid construction must not create counter gaps. | Post-super validation was rejected. | — |
| D-003 | Use nominal Station resolution in `dock_at()`. | Runtime protocols check attribute presence, not the Station-specific semantic contract. | Runtime-checkable protocol dispatch was rejected. | ADR-0011 |
| D-004 | Make End Turn accept an inactive resolved Starship. | The approved action has no active-state precondition; game-over belongs to state control. | Adding an inactive guard was rejected. | ADR-0005 |
| D-005 | Collapse missing and wrong-type player resolution under `InactiveEntityError` for v0.1. | Both are construction-time impossibilities with the same current response. | A new exception root conflicted with SPEC-S005. | — |
| D-006 | Accept synchronous receiver-exception propagation in v0.1. | One known-safe bridge subscriber bounds the risk. | A nonexistent library helper and premature custom dispatch were rejected. | — |

> **§8.4 (Solution Alternatives Considered) is Full-tier** and is intentionally omitted at the Standard profile.

### 8.5 Design Constraints

- AP mutation is owned only by Starship.
- Action precondition and effect order is normative.
- Every successful event follows the complete corresponding mutation.
- Station and Starship form one runtime edge; Starship and TurnManager form one runtime edge in the opposite layer direction without a backedge.
- Later combat modules remain absent even from `TYPE_CHECKING`.

> **§8.6 (Dependency Policy) is Full-tier** and is intentionally omitted at the Standard profile.

## 10. Behavior and Workflows

### 10.1 Primary Workflow

1. The wire-up layer constructs and adds a validated Starship.
2. Movement or Dock validates every precondition, debits AP, mutates required state, and emits the corresponding post-state event.
3. End Turn resolves the player by ID, restores AP, increments the counter, and emits the incremented turn.
4. Subscribers observe the updated model synchronously.

### 10.2 Alternate Workflows

| ID | Trigger | Behavior | Expected Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AW-001 | The player Starship is inactive at End Turn. | Restore AP, increment, and emit normally. | End Turn remains available for state/controller handling. |
| AW-002 | A move or Dock precondition fails. | Raise the first ordered typed error without mutation or event. | The caller can report one deterministic failure. |
| AW-003 | A receiver raises during `turn_advanced.send()`. | The exception propagates after AP and turn mutation; later receivers may be skipped. | The v0.1 bounded risk is visible and not retried automatically. |

### 10.3 Edge Cases

| ID | Edge Case | Expected Behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EC-001 | Movement target equals the current position. | `NotAdjacentError` is raised before AP debit. |
| EC-002 | AP cost is zero, negative, bool, float, or another non-int. | The approved domain error is raised without changing AP. |
| EC-003 | Player ID resolves to a Station. | `InactiveEntityError` is raised without restoration, increment, or event. |

### 10.4 State Transitions

TurnManager has an integer turn progression rather than a formal state machine: each successful call advances `current_turn` by exactly one after AP restoration. Game-state transitions remain owned by SPEC-S008.

#### Purpose

Two modules that close the v0.1 model layer's **action surface** — the player entity plus the turn orchestrator:

- `model/entities/starship.py` — `Starship(GameObject)`: kwarg-only constructor with `kind` discriminator, `ship_class`, `hull`, `ap_max`, `ap_remaining`. Implements all three v0.1 action methods (`move_to`, `dock_at`, `restore_ap`) plus the internal atomic check-then-debit `_debit_ap(cost)` helper required by SPEC-ML01 FR-005.
- `model/combat/turn_manager.py` — `TurnManager(player_id, *, current_turn=1)`: turn-counter + orchestrator. `advance_turn(sector_map)` resolves the player, restores AP, increments the turn, and emits `turn_advanced` per SPEC-ML01 FR-008/FR-009. Owns no AP, no NPC roster, and no initiative.

They land together because (a) `combat/turn_manager.py` runtime-imports `entities.starship.Starship` to do `isinstance(player, Starship)` before `restore_ap()` — `TurnManager` literally cannot ship without `Starship`; (b) both implement the SPEC-ML01 FR-005 through FR-009 action contracts, and bundling them surfaces ordering and error-type decisions a per-module spec would miss; and (c) together they provide the **model-layer prerequisites** for `DESIGN.md` section 10.1 DoD items #5, #6, #7. The DoD items themselves require view/controller wire-up (rendering, click-to-move handlers, Dock button state, End Turn button, comm log) which is out of scope for Step 7 and lands in later integration work. Step 7 completes the model-side surface; the user-visible DoD remains gated on that UI integration.

This spec also surfaced and amended five inconsistencies in `v0.1-model-layer.md`, including one research-found blocker: the former Runtime DAG classified `entities.station` as type-only for `entities.starship`, but `dock_at()` performs `isinstance(target, Station)` at runtime. The complete amendment evidence remains under **Cross-references and the umbrella amendments**.

The asymmetry is intentional.
- Module + class docstrings mandatory on public types. Method docstrings only where the _why_ is non-obvious (action precondition ordering, `_debit_ap` atomicity, `advance_turn` mutation order).
- No new `.importlinter` contracts. The existing four contracts (`model-is-qt-free`, `view-does-not-import-model-events`, `projection-is-qt-free`, `blinker-only-in-events`) cover step-7 surface without additions.

```python
class Starship(GameObject):
    """Active player/NPC entity on a sector grid.

    Owns AP (SPEC-ML01 FR-005) and the three v0.1 action methods. All
    cross-module entity references are by EntityId (SPEC-ML01 DR-001).
    """

    kind: ClassVar[str] = "starship"
```

**`kind: ClassVar[str] = "starship"`** matches SPEC-S006's `Station.kind` convention for consistency. Historical research at [`docs/research/2026-05-25-v0.1-step-7-open-questions.md`](../research/2026-05-25-v0.1-step-7-open-questions.md) section Q3 recommended `Final[str]` for stronger no-override enforcement in the then-current checker, but migrating both classes together is a cross-cutting concern deferred under **Still open (v0.2 concerns, NOT v0.1 blockers)**. v0.1 has no Starship subclasses, so the override risk is theoretical.

##### Constructor signature

```python
def __init__(
    self,
    *,
    position: GridPosition,
    ship_class: str,
    hull: int,
    ap_max: int = 5,
    ap_remaining: int | None = None,
) -> None:
    ...
```

**Keyword-only `__init__`** (leading `*` after `self`): five parameters, four of which are scalars whose positional order is not obvious at the call site. `Starship(pos, "constitution", 100, 5, 5)` reads worse than `Starship(position=pos, ship_class="constitution", hull=100)`. This matches SPEC-S006 IR-002.

**`ap_remaining: int | None = None`** — sentinel pattern. If the caller omits or passes `None`, `__init__` sets `self.ap_remaining = ap_max` after validation. v0.1 callers always omit so turn 1 starts full per SPEC-ML01 FR-005. v0.2 save/load can pass the persisted value explicitly (`Starship(..., ap_remaining=3)`) without an API break.

**`ship_class: str` is open**, not a `Literal["constitution", "miranda", ...]`. Research finding ([open-questions section Q-F → cross-ref spec-assumptions section F]) corroborated by step-6's wide-Literal/narrow-runtime pattern: `Literal` is appropriate for closed enumerations (`Station.station_type`), but fights open-registry fields like `ship_class` where the set is extensible by mods, v0.2 ship-class registry, future ship classes. v0.1 validates only non-emptiness; the wide-Literal/narrow-runtime pattern is deliberately _not_ used here.

##### Validation pipeline + EntityId-non-consumption invariant

All five validation checks run **before** `super().__init__(position)`. This preserves the contiguous-monotone `_entity_id_counter`; `GameObject.__init__()` remains the caller of allocator function `_next_entity_id()`. The ordering mirrors SPEC-S006 FR-010 and the SA-002 identity-preservation outcome. The full constructor body:

```python
def __init__(
    self,
    *,
    position: GridPosition,
    ship_class: str,
    hull: int,
    ap_max: int = 5,
    ap_remaining: int | None = None,
) -> None:
    # All validation BEFORE super().__init__() so failed construction does
    # NOT advance _entity_id_counter (mirrors SPEC-S006 FR-010).
    if not isinstance(ship_class, str):
        raise TypeError(
            f"ship_class must be str, got {type(ship_class).__name__}"
        )
    if len(ship_class) == 0:
        raise ValueError("ship_class must be non-empty")

    if not isinstance(hull, int) or isinstance(hull, bool):
        raise TypeError(f"hull must be int, got {type(hull).__name__}")
    if hull < 1:
        raise ValueError(f"hull must be >= 1, got {hull}")

    if not isinstance(ap_max, int) or isinstance(ap_max, bool):
        raise TypeError(f"ap_max must be int, got {type(ap_max).__name__}")
    if ap_max < 1:
        raise ValueError(f"ap_max must be >= 1, got {ap_max}")

    if ap_remaining is not None:
        if not isinstance(ap_remaining, int) or isinstance(ap_remaining, bool):
            raise TypeError(
                f"ap_remaining must be int or None, got {type(ap_remaining).__name__}"
            )
        if not (0 <= ap_remaining <= ap_max):
            raise ValueError(
                f"ap_remaining must be in [0, {ap_max}], got {ap_remaining}"
            )

    super().__init__(position)  # ID allocation happens here
    self.ship_class: str = ship_class
    self.hull: int = hull
    self.ap_max: int = ap_max
    self.ap_remaining: int = ap_max if ap_remaining is None else ap_remaining
```

| Field | Type check | Range check | Type-error | Range-error |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ship_class` | `isinstance(ship_class, str)` (`str` excludes `bytes` natively) | `len(ship_class) > 0` | `TypeError(f"ship_class must be str, got {type(...).__name__}")` | `ValueError("ship_class must be non-empty")` |
| `hull` | non-bool `int` | `hull >= 1` | `TypeError(f"hull must be int, got {type(...).__name__}")` | `ValueError(f"hull must be >= 1, got {hull}")` |
| `ap_max` | non-bool `int` | `ap_max >= 1` | `TypeError(f"ap_max must be int, got {type(...).__name__}")` | `ValueError(f"ap_max must be >= 1, got {ap_max}")` |
| `ap_remaining` (when not `None`) | non-bool `int` | `0 <= ap_remaining <= ap_max` | `TypeError(f"ap_remaining must be int or None, got {type(...).__name__}")` | `ValueError(f"ap_remaining must be in [0, {ap_max}], got {ap_remaining}")` |

**Why `hull >= 1`** (research open-questions section H and spec-assumptions section H): a `hull == 0` ship at construction is incoherent — `active=True` from `GameObject.__init__` but semantically dead. The `tcod` tutorial uses the same `max_hp >= 1` invariant. Combat (v0.2) handles damage-to-zero through a later setter that also updates active state, preserving the named SPEC-ML01 active-entity reachability invariant.

**Why `ap_max >= 1`**: parallel reasoning — an `ap_max == 0` ship can never act, so accepting it at construction would let the wire-up layer spawn a dead ship that fails every action method silently.

**Why `ValueError`, not a model exception**: constructor validation uses `ValueError`, matching SPEC-S003 FR-002 and SPEC-S006 FR-010/FR-011. No model exception class fits — `IllegalActionError` subclasses are for action preconditions on already constructed entities; this is "the value you passed me to construct with is wrong."

##### `_debit_ap(cost)` helper — atomic check-then-debit

```python
def _debit_ap(self, cost: int) -> None:
    """Internal AP-debit primitive. Atomic check-then-debit.

    cost must be a non-bool int >= 1 (the action layer's narrowest
    sensible domain; cost <= 0 has no game meaning and cost=True would
    silently work via Python's bool-is-int subtyping — the same
    footgun mitigation step-6 SectorMap.__init__ applies to its axes).

    Raise BEFORE mutation: if cost > self.ap_remaining, raise
    InsufficientAPError and leave self.ap_remaining unchanged. Else
    decrement. v0.1 callers always pass cost=1; v0.2's variable-cost
    moves (Pythagorean diagonal per DESIGN.md section 5.1) pass the computed
    cost — at which point an off-by-one or sign bug in the cost
    formula MUST surface as ValueError, not silently restore AP.

    Leading underscore: action methods (move_to, dock_at) are the only
    intended callers. The defensive cost validation costs ~4 lines
    and shipped now because the helper is the v0.2 variable-cost
    extension point (review finding SA-003).
    """
    # v0.2 TODO: if Starship instances are mutated from multiple
    # QThreadPool workers (AI move tasks), this read-modify-write becomes
    # a race. Two paths (see DESIGN.md section 9.4):
    #   (a) Confine all model mutations to a single "model thread" and
    #       use Qt queued signal/slot for cross-thread handoff
    #       (lock-free, idiomatic Qt — preferred).
    #   (b) Wrap with threading.Lock()/QMutex around the
    #       check + decrement.
    # PEP 703 free-threading makes this real even without QThreadPool
    # once 3.14 free-threaded builds are adopted.
    if not isinstance(cost, int) or isinstance(cost, bool):
        raise TypeError(f"cost must be int, got {type(cost).__name__}")
    if cost < 1:
        raise ValueError(f"cost must be >= 1, got {cost}")

    if cost > self.ap_remaining:
        raise InsufficientAPError(required=cost, available=self.ap_remaining)
    self.ap_remaining -= cost
```

**Why raise-before-mutate** (research-grounded per [spec-assumptions section A](../research/2026-05-25-v0.1-step-7-spec-assumptions.md)): universal convention across `esper`, `tcod-ecs`, and the roguelike-development corpus. Debit-then-check-negative is broken and undocumented as valid anywhere.

**Why a helper, not inline check + decrement in each action method** (Q3 from brainstorming): two action methods (`move_to`, `dock_at`) would duplicate the check+decrement pattern. Single point of truth eliminates a "future caller forgets to check" footgun. v0.1 callers always pass `cost=1`; the parameter exists as the v0.2 variable-cost-move extension point.

**Why cost is validated as non-bool int `>= 1`** (review finding SA-003): the helper is the v0.2 variable-cost extension point. A future buggy cost computation that produces `0` (no-op), `-1` (silently restores AP via `ap_remaining -= -1`), `True` (silently 1 via bool-is-int), or `1.5` (TypeError on subtraction in v0.1 but undefined under PEP 604 union widening in v0.2) must surface as a deterministic exception at this seam, not silently corrupt AP. The cost validation mirrors step-6 SectorMap's axis-validation pattern (int/bool/range, same exception types).

##### `move_to(target, sector_map)` — implements SPEC-ML01 FR-006

```python
def move_to(self, target: GridPosition, sector_map: SectorMap) -> None:
    """Move this ship one cell. Implements SPEC-ML01 FR-006.

    Preconditions (checked in this order; first failure raises):
      1. self.active                     → InactiveEntityError
      2. sector_map.bounds_check(target) → OutOfBoundsError
      3. self.position.is_adjacent(target) → NotAdjacentError
      4. self.ap_remaining >= 1            → InsufficientAPError (via _debit_ap)

    Effects (ordered):
      1. Capture from_position before mutation.
      2. _debit_ap(1) — may raise; if so, no mutation occurs.
      3. self.position = target.
      4. Emit ship_moved with payload carrying from_position + target.

    Zero-distance moves (target == self.position) raise NotAdjacentError
    because is_adjacent requires chebyshev_distance == 1 (chebyshev=0 is
    not adjacent). This is roguelike convention per research
    spec-assumptions section B.
    """
    if not self.active:
        raise InactiveEntityError(entity_id=self.id)
    if not sector_map.bounds_check(target):
        raise OutOfBoundsError(
            position=target,
            sector_dims=(sector_map.width, sector_map.height, sector_map.depth),
        )
    if not self.position.is_adjacent(target):
        raise NotAdjacentError(from_position=self.position, to_position=target)

    from_position = self.position
    self._debit_ap(1)  # may raise InsufficientAPError; no mutation if so
    self.position = target
    ship_moved.send(
        self,
        payload=ShipMovedPayload(
            ship_id=self.id,
            from_position=from_position,
            to_position=target,
        ),
    )
```

**Why `from_position` is captured before `_debit_ap`**: if `_debit_ap` raises, the local has no effect (Python doesn't leak names from a raising statement back to the caller frame's local namespace). Capturing earlier than strictly necessary also documents intent — `from_position` is the _pre-mutation_ position, full stop.

**Why preconditions are in this order** (SPEC-ML01 FR-006): `active` is the cheapest and most fundamental check; `bounds_check` is next; `is_adjacent` is geometric; AP debit is last because it is the only successful precondition step that mutates state. Earlier failures therefore cannot consume AP.

##### `dock_at(station_id, sector_map)` — implements SPEC-ML01 FR-007

```python
def dock_at(self, station_id: EntityId, sector_map: SectorMap) -> None:
    """Dock at a station. Implements SPEC-ML01 FR-007.

    Preconditions (checked in this order; first failure raises):
      1. self.active                                       → InactiveEntityError
      2. target resolvable AND isinstance(Station) AND active → NotDockableError (conflated)
      3. self.position.is_adjacent(target.position)        → NotAdjacentError
      4. target.accepts_dock(self) is True                 → NotDockableError
      5. self.ap_remaining >= 1                            → InsufficientAPError (via _debit_ap)

    Effects (ordered):
      1. _debit_ap(1) — may raise; if so, no mutation occurs.
      2. Emit docked with payload carrying ship_id + station_id.

    No state on the Station mutates (SPEC-ML01 FR-007). Service application
    is mission-system-side in later versions; v0.1 only proves the action
    pipeline. The conflated NotDockableError (covering stale-ID, wrong-type,
    and inactive-station) is intentional per SPEC-ML01 FR-007 — the
    controller does not distinguish the sub-failures in v0.1 UI messaging.
    """
    if not self.active:
        raise InactiveEntityError(entity_id=self.id)

    target = sector_map.get(station_id)
    if target is None or not isinstance(target, Station) or not target.active:
        raise NotDockableError(ship_id=self.id, station_id=station_id)

    if not self.position.is_adjacent(target.position):
        raise NotAdjacentError(from_position=self.position, to_position=target.position)

    if not target.accepts_dock(self):
        raise NotDockableError(ship_id=self.id, station_id=station_id)

    self._debit_ap(1)
    docked.send(self, payload=DockedPayload(ship_id=self.id, station_id=station_id))
```

**Why nominal `isinstance(target, Station)`, not a `_DockTarget: Protocol`** (research [spec-assumptions section C](../research/2026-05-25-v0.1-step-7-spec-assumptions.md)): `accepts_dock` is a `Station`-specific method called immediately after the check. `@runtime_checkable` Protocol `isinstance` checks attribute _existence_ only, not method signatures — anything with an `accepts_dock` attribute (even a wrong-signature one) would pass. Nominal typing here documents the actual contract more honestly than structural typing. The Station-side asymmetry (`accepts_dock(ship: _Dockable)`) is correct in its direction because the predicate genuinely reads only `ship.active`.

**Why `target.accepts_dock(self)` is called** even though v0.1 reduces it to `ship.active` (already true after the first precondition): SPEC-ML01 FR-007 and SPEC-S006 FR-013 lock the predicate as the v0.2 extension point. Calling it now means the v0.2 faction/reputation widening remains conjunctive on the Station side (`return ship.active and faction_ok(ship)`); `dock_at()` need not change.

##### `restore_ap()`

```python
def restore_ap(self) -> None:
    """Reset ap_remaining to ap_max. Called by TurnManager.advance_turn
    on the player at the start of each turn (SPEC-ML01 FR-008).

    Unconditional: there is no v0.1 mechanism for partial restoration or
    decay. Per DESIGN.md section 3.1, unused AP does not carry over.
    """
    self.ap_remaining = self.ap_max
```

##### What's deliberately not on `Starship`

- **No `Faction` field, no shields/weapons/sensors/hull-damage handling.** The SPEC-ML01 preserved module surface and `DESIGN.md` section 10.1 defer them to v0.2.
- **No `__eq__` override.** Inherits SPEC-S003's identity equality. Two Starships at the same position with identical attributes are not equal — they are different ships.
- **No `combat.turn_manager` import.** The `TurnManager → Starship` runtime edge is one-directional under NFR-001. A backedge would cycle.
- **No `Faction` validation against a registry.** Open-registry pattern per research [open-questions section F → spec-assumptions section F].
- **No `damage(amount)`, `heal(amount)`, or `set_hull(value)` methods.** v0.2 combat owns hull mutation.

##### `advance_turn(sector_map)` — implements SPEC-ML01 FR-008/FR-009

```python
def advance_turn(self, sector_map: SectorMap) -> None:
    """End the current turn. Implements SPEC-ML01 FR-008/FR-009.

    Preconditions: none — End Turn is always available, including when
    the player ship is inactive (game-over state). The umbrella locks
    this: dead-ship End Turn restores AP (harmless — an inactive ship
    cannot consume AP via move_to/dock_at), increments the counter,
    and emits the event. The controller detects game-over via state
    transitions, not via advance_turn refusing to run.

    Effects (ordered, per SPEC-ML01 FR-008 and NFR-002):
      1. Resolve player from sector_map.
      2. Verify the resolution succeeded AND the resolved entity is a
         Starship instance; raise InactiveEntityError if either fails.
         This is type-and-presence verification only — it does NOT
         check player.active. UNREACHABLE in v0.1 by construction (the
         player ship is added to SectorMap at session start and never
         removed); the check hardens against v0.2 persistence-layer
         foot-guns where a stale player_id could survive a save/load
         round-trip without its referent.
      3. player.restore_ap() — sets player.ap_remaining = player.ap_max.
         Runs even if player.active is False.
      4. self.current_turn += 1.
      5. Emit turn_advanced with payload carrying the incremented turn number.

    Subscribers reading manager.current_turn inside a turn_advanced
    handler see the incremented turn (SPEC-ML01 NFR-002). See the preserved
    synchronous-send exception risk
    for the blinker synchronous-send exception-propagation behavior.
    """
    player = sector_map.get(self.player_id)
    if player is None or not isinstance(player, Starship):
        raise InactiveEntityError(entity_id=self.player_id)

    player.restore_ap()
    self.current_turn += 1
    turn_advanced.send(
        self,
        payload=TurnAdvancedPayload(turn_number=self.current_turn),
    )
```

**Why `InactiveEntityError` for the None / non-Starship case** (SPEC-ML01 FR-008/FR-009): the semantic mismatch ("missing" or "wrong-type" framed as "inactive") is mild but real. The v0.1 collapse is acceptable because both branches are unreachable by construction and the controller response is identical. The possible v0.2 split remains under **Still open (v0.2 concerns, NOT v0.1 blockers)** and would add `MissingEntityError(IllegalActionError)` as a peer under SPEC-S005's existing root, not as a `LookupError` refactor. An inactive Starship is not one of the collapsed failures; SA-001 and FR-013 require it to succeed.

**Why effect order is `restore → increment → emit`** (SPEC-ML01 FR-008 and NFR-002): subscribers reading `manager.current_turn` inside the `turn_advanced` handler must see the new value. Emitting before incrementing would make the payload and manager temporarily disagree. SPEC-S005's post-mutation event contract applies the same rule to `state_changed`.

**Synchronous-send exception risk** (research open-questions section Q2): blinker's `send()` propagates a receiver exception and skips later receivers while `current_turn` is already incremented. This is documented behavior, not a bug. v0.1 has exactly one known-safe subscriber, so the bounded risk is accepted. `blinker.send_robust()` does **not** exist (PRs #3 and #27 were proposed but never merged). The preserved v0.2 mitigation is a local `_send_robust(signal, sender, **kw)` utility that iterates `signal.receivers_for(sender)`, catches per receiver, returns `(receiver, value_or_exception)` tuples, and logs caught exceptions at `ERROR` with loguru. Trigger: a second non-bridge subscriber lands. The umbrella risk amendment is preserved below.

##### What's deliberately not on `TurnManager`

- **No `debit_player_ap` method.** AP lives on the entity per SPEC-ML01 FR-005. Action methods own AP mutation.
- **No NPC roster, no initiative.** Single-player v0.1; v0.2 generalizes per `DESIGN.md` section 5.2 combat.
- **No event subscription.** `TurnManager` is a publisher only; it does not connect to any signal.
- **No try/except around `turn_advanced.send`.** v0.1 accepts blinker's synchronous-propagation contract because the subscriber set is one and known safe. WH-005 preserves the local robust-dispatch trigger.
- **No `tick()` / `time` field.** v0.1 turns are discrete; no real-time clock. `DESIGN.md` section 3.1 locks discrete-turn semantics.

#### Invariants

Properties step 7 contributes that the rest of the model layer relies on; reviewer should probe these.

1. **`Starship.kind`** is the class-level string `"starship"` for every Starship instance — the later TOML save-format discriminator preserved by SPEC-ML01 DR-002.
2. **EntityId non-consumption on failed Starship construction**: `Starship.__init__` validates all arguments before calling `super().__init__(position)`. Failed construction does not advance `_entity_id_counter`; successful `GameObject` construction still allocates through `_next_entity_id()`. This mirrors SPEC-S006 FR-010.
3. **`_debit_ap` is atomic check-then-debit**: raises `InsufficientAPError` BEFORE mutation; `ap_remaining` is never decremented on a failed debit.
4. **`_debit_ap` cost domain is non-bool int `>= 1`**: any other input raises `TypeError`/`ValueError` BEFORE the check-then-debit body runs; `ap_remaining` is unchanged. Closes the v0.2-variable-cost extension point against silent AP-restoration via negative cost (SA-003 review finding).
5. **`Starship.ap_remaining ∈ [0, ap_max]`** after every action method returns (successful or failed). No action method can drive `ap_remaining` negative or above `ap_max`. Together with the preceding `_debit_ap` cost-domain invariant, this prevents any caller, including a future variable-cost caller, from widening the AP range.
6. **`move_to` precondition order is locked**: active → bounds → adjacent → AP. First failure raises and no mutation occurs (no `ap_remaining` decrement, no `position` change). The order is enforced by the implementation, not just convention.
7. **`dock_at` precondition order is locked**: active → resolve-target-is-active-Station → adjacent → accepts_dock → AP. First failure raises and no mutation occurs. The Station's state never mutates during `dock_at` in v0.1.
8. **`advance_turn` effect order is locked**: resolve → restore → increment → emit per SPEC-ML01 FR-008 and NFR-002. Subscribers see the incremented turn and restored AP. The effect order holds for inactive players too under SPEC-ML01 FR-009 and SA-001.
9. **`TurnManager → Starship` is the only new runtime DAG edge in step 7** (in addition to `Starship → Station`). No back-edge from `Starship` to `TurnManager`; the property is enforced by a regex-grep test in `test_starship.py` (no-back-edge row) and a positive-import assertion in `test_turn_manager.py` (import-structure row).
10. **`Starship` and `TurnManager` import nothing from `combat.combat_resolver` or `combat.ai_state_machine`** — those modules do not exist in v0.1 and even a guarded forward reference would violate NFR-001 and strict static analysis.
11. **`turn_advanced.send` is synchronous**, and any receiver exception propagates out while `current_turn` is already incremented. v0.1 has one known-safe subscriber, so the bounded risk is accepted; WH-005 preserves the trigger for a local `_send_robust` utility. The library exposes no `blinker.send_robust`.

#### Open questions

##### Locked decisions

- **Two-module bundle, one PR.** Both modules implement SPEC-ML01 FR-005 through FR-009; landing them together unblocks the model prerequisites for DoD #5/#6/#7. This mirrors SPEC-S006's bundling rationale.
- **Tight defensive validation on all Starship scalars.** All four fields (`ship_class`, `hull`, `ap_max`, `ap_remaining`) get type and range checks before `super().__init__()`. SPEC-S006 FR-010 is generalized to Starship.
- **`_debit_ap(cost)` is atomic check-then-debit.** Raises `InsufficientAPError` before mutation. Single point of truth for AP debit; v0.1 callers always pass `cost=1`; v0.2 variable-cost moves pass computed costs. Research-grounded per [spec-assumptions section A](../research/2026-05-25-v0.1-step-7-spec-assumptions.md).
- **`TurnManager(player_id, *, current_turn: int = 1)`** with both fields validated as non-bool int `>= 1`. v0.2 save/load reconstructs without API break.
- **`ship_class` is open `str`, non-empty validation only** ([open-questions section F → spec-assumptions section F]). Open-registry pattern; wide-Literal/narrow-runtime (step-6 `Station.station_type`) is _not_ used here because the set is extensible.
- **`hull >= 1` and `ap_max >= 1` lower bounds** ([open-questions section H + parallel reasoning]). A hull-0 or ap_max-0 ship at construction is incoherent. v0.2 damage-to-zero handled via setter + `active=False` flip.
- **`isinstance(target, Station)` nominal dispatch in `dock_at`** ([spec-assumptions section C]). `@runtime_checkable` Protocol `isinstance` checks attribute existence only — false-safety trap. Nominal is correct here because the predicate calls a `Station`-specific method.
- **`entities.station` is a RUNTIME import in `entities.starship`** (research spec-assumptions section D). The former umbrella Runtime DAG classified it as type-only, contradicting the runtime `isinstance` in `dock_at()`. The reconciliation is preserved below.
- **`InactiveEntityError` for None / non-Starship player in `advance_turn`** (SPEC-ML01 FR-008). It is a semantic stretch; the possible v0.2 peer exception remains in the first still-open item.
- **`TurnManager` does not have validate-before-super** ([open-questions section E → spec-assumptions section E]). Not a `GameObject` subclass; `object.__init__` is side-effect-free. Plain top-to-bottom validation.
- **`v0.2 _send_robust` is a local utility, not `blinker.send_robust`** ([open-questions section Q2]). `blinker.send_robust` does not exist — PRs #3 and #27 in `pallets-eco/blinker` were never merged. Trigger: second non-bridge subscriber lands.
- **`kind: ClassVar[str]` (matching step 6), not `Final[str]`** ([open-questions section Q3]). `Final[str]` is research-recommended but inconsistent with step-6's locked pattern. See still-open 3.
- **v0.2 threading TODO comment in `_debit_ap`** (research open-questions section Q4). PEP 703 free-threading plus QThreadPool AI workers make AP debit a v0.2 race condition. The preserved helper listing retains the comment.
- **Test probe class shapes are implementation-defined**, not spec-locked (research open-questions section Q5). The preserved test obligations describe probe usage rather than mandating private test helpers.

##### Still open (v0.2 concerns, NOT v0.1 blockers)

1. **`InactiveEntityError` vs. eventual `MissingEntityError(IllegalActionError)` split.** `TurnManager.advance_turn` collapses **two** sub-failures (player None / non-Starship) under `InactiveEntityError`. The exception name is a semantic stretch; an inactive player is not a failure under SA-001, FR-013, and the preserved `advance_turn` effect-order invariant. The mismatch is v0.1-acceptable because both collapsed failures are unreachable by construction. **v0.2 path**: introduce `MissingEntityError(IllegalActionError)` as a peer under SPEC-S005's existing `ModelError` root, not as a `LookupError` refactor. Trigger: bridge error-to-UI mapping needs distinct user messages. Round-2 review caught the former stale three-sub-failure wording.

2. **`v0.2 _send_robust` utility design and trigger condition.** v0.1's plain `turn_advanced.send(...)` accepts blinker's documented exception-propagation contract. v0.2 must implement a local `_send_robust(signal, sender, **kw)` utility (since `blinker.send_robust` does not exist). The Django pattern: iterate `signal.receivers_for(sender)` with per-receiver `try/except Exception`, append `(receiver, value-or-exception)` tuples to a results list, log caught exceptions at `ERROR` level via `loguru`. **Trigger**: a second non-bridge subscriber connects to any model signal (e.g., replay log subscribes to `turn_advanced`, telemetry subscribes to `ship_moved`). The first cross-subscriber dispatch is when fault isolation matters.

3. **`kind: ClassVar[str]` → `kind: Final[str]` migration deferred** (research open-questions section Q3). Historical research recommended `Final[str]` for stronger no-override enforcement and cited MyPy bug #18235. Migrating only Starship while Station remains `ClassVar[str]` is inconsistent. **Current trigger**: either the cross-cutting form is supported cleanly under `[tool.basedpyright]`, or both Starship and Station are converted to dataclasses. The amendment must migrate both together.

4. **Threading model selection for v0.2** ([open-questions section Q4]). Two options for v0.2 AP-race mitigation:
   - **(A) Single model-thread affinity (preferred)**: all entity state changes happen on one thread; AI workers compute decisions and post results back via Qt queued signals. Lock-free, idiomatic Qt.
   - **(B) Per-entity `threading.Lock` / `QMutex`**: wraps OS mutex; interoperable between Python threads and QThreads. Correct but adds contention.

   v0.1's `_debit_ap` TODO comment documents both paths. The choice is owned by the v0.2 combat spec.

## 12. Error Handling and Recovery

### 12.1 Expected Failures

| ID | Failure Mode | User/System Behavior | Logging / Observability | Recovery |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ERR-001 | Starship or TurnManager construction input is invalid. | The approved `TypeError` or `ValueError` is raised before usable state or entity identity is created. | Constructor and counter-preservation tests capture every branch. | Correct the caller or loaded value and construct again. |
| ERR-002 | A movement precondition fails. | The first ordered typed action error is raised without AP, position, or event mutation. | Failure and dual-violation tests capture the order. | Correct the state or choose a valid adjacent target. |
| ERR-003 | A Dock precondition fails. | The first ordered typed action error is raised without AP, Station, or event mutation. | Failure and dual-violation tests capture the order. | Correct ship/target state or choose a valid Station. |
| ERR-004 | End Turn resolves no Starship. | `InactiveEntityError` is raised without restoration, increment, or event. | TurnManager defensive tests capture missing and wrong-type results. | Repair the world/player-ID relationship before retrying. |
| ERR-005 | A synchronous turn-event receiver raises. | The exception propagates after AP and turn mutation; later receivers may be skipped. | The preserved risk and event contract expose the partial-delivery state. | The caller handles the exception; no automatic retry is declared. |

### 12.2 Retry and Idempotency

Constructor failures and action precondition failures are safe to retry after correction because they do not mutate. Successful movement, Dock, and End Turn are not idempotent: repeating them debits AP, emits another event, or advances another turn. Event sends must not be replayed independently of their owning mutation.

### 12.3 Rollback / Recovery

Precondition failures require no rollback. Receiver failures occur after mutation by design; v0.1 propagates them without rollback. Recovery inspects the already-updated Starship/TurnManager state and must not repeat the action blindly.
